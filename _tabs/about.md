## What I do

Since 2017 I have been part of the [BIT SENTINEL](https://bit-sentinel.com/){:target="_blank"} team, working on Red Teaming, Penetration Testing, Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) and Incident Response projects.

Outside of client work, most of my time goes into security competitions — organising them, training for them, and writing the challenges:

- **Romanian Cyber Security Challenge (ROCSC)** — organiser and trainer since 2018.
